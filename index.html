Weekly Planner Guide

Functions, statuses, history, syncing, and keyboard shortcuts

Calendar basics

Week / Day: switch between the full seven-day calendar and a focused single-day view. The arrow buttons move one week in Week view and one day in Day view. Today returns you to the current date.

Repeated Tasks are routines that appear every day. Today's Tasks are one-off tasks tied permanently to one specific date. Both sections can be folded independently; their star ratings remain visible even while folded.

Due is for date-specific deadlines/reminders. Due entries have their own red, orange, blue and green statuses and expand automatically for longer text.

Creating & managing tasks

+ Add Task creates either a repeated task or a one-off task. The + button inside a day creates a one-off task for that date. You can choose an order position when creating it.

Open a task to edit its title, change its position, change status, and write Notes / Instructions. Notes on repeated tasks are shared across every day; notes on one-off tasks belong to that dated task.

Reorder: on desktop, drag tasks within their section. You can also use ▲/▼ or type an exact position number. Reordering a date does not rewrite the order of other dates.

Priority ⭐: marks an important task with a subtle gold star and places it toward the top of its section.

Reschedule: one-off tasks can be moved to Tomorrow, Next week, or any chosen date. Their task information moves with them.

Duplicate: creates another copy of a task, preserving its notes and priority. Repeated tasks duplicate as repeated tasks; one-off tasks duplicate on their date.

Delete: removes the task from the active calendar. Historical status information and archived task metadata are retained so lifetime statistics and search history remain useful.

Status colors

Not StartedDefault state. An untouched eligible task is treated as not done for historical counting.
Act NowIn progress and currently requires your attention.
WaitIn progress but waiting on another person, event, or dependency.
CompleteFinished. Each green task occurrence contributes to your completion totals.

Each repeated task/day combination is its own occurrence. Completing the same routine on five different days therefore records five completions.

Backlog

Drag between calendar and backlog: one-off calendar tasks can be dragged into the Backlog, and backlog items can be dragged onto a day to schedule them. You can also drag one-off tasks directly from one day to another. Repeated routines remain daily and therefore are not moved between dates.

Backlog ordering: drag backlog items to reorder them or move them between categories. Drag category headers to rearrange the categories themselves.

The Backlog stores things you want to remember without assigning them to a day yet. Organize items into named sections, rename sections, delete sections, and drag items to reorder or move them between sections.

Schedule a backlog item directly to Tomorrow, This week, or a chosen Date. Scheduling turns it into a dated one-off calendar task and removes it from the backlog.

Search

Search looks through active task names and task notes. Archived/deleted task metadata is also searchable when it has been retained. Selecting a live result takes you to the relevant part of the calendar.

Ratings, totals & weekly review

Section stars: the five stars under Repeated Tasks and Today's Tasks are independent daily ratings. They remain visible when a section is folded.

Lifetime totals: show how many repeated-task occurrences and daily-task occurrences have ever been marked green. There is no denominator or completion percentage.

This Week: counts green completions during the current week and compares that number with last week.

Week Summary: gives a positive review of the selected week: total completed, repeated completed, daily completed, priority tasks completed, and the most productive day.

Milestones: brief celebrations appear when your combined lifetime completions cross 100, 250, 500, 1,000, 2,500, 5,000 and later milestones. A milestone is only celebrated once.

Saving, cloud sync & backups

Your planner saves locally and syncs its calendar state to your Supabase account. The status pill shows states such as Saving…, Synced, Offline, or Sync error.

The ⋯ menu contains manual cloud refresh, JSON backup/restore, and sign-out controls. A downloaded backup is an extra safety copy of your planner state. Your Week/Day view preference is device-local.

Replacing the five GitHub app files updates the planner code; it does not itself replace your calendar data stored in Supabase.

Keyboard shortcuts

/Open Search
NCreate a new task
TJump to Today
BOpen / close Backlog
←Previous day or week
→Next day or week
EscClose task/search/review/info popups

Shortcuts are ignored while you are typing in an input, textarea, or dropdown, so they won't interfere with task names or notes.

Useful distinctions

Repeated task: an ongoing routine. Today's task: something belonging to one date. Due item: a deadline/reminder. Backlog item: something not scheduled yet. Priority: an importance marker, not a status.
